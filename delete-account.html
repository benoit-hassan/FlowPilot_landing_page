Delete directly from the app

The fastest way to delete your account and all associated data is directly from within FlowPilot:

  1. Open FlowPilot
  2. Tap the menu icon (☰) in the top-right corner
  3. Go to Account
  4. Scroll down to Danger zone
  5. Tap Delete account and confirm

This immediately and permanently deletes all your sessions, categories, schedule blocks, settings, and your account credentials.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, send us an email and we will delete your account and all associated data within 7 days.

supportflowpilot@gmail.com

Please include the email address associated with your FlowPilot account in your message.

What gets deleted

  1. All timer sessions and history
  2. All categories and quick timers
  3. All schedule blocks
  4. Your account settings and preferences
  5. Your authentication credentials

Premium purchases are processed by Stripe and are not refundable once the account is deleted. If you have questions about billing, contact us before deleting your account.
